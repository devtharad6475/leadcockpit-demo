# Pricing and Minimum Order Policy

## The rule that overrides everything else

**Nobody quotes a price except Rakesh or Priya, in writing.**

This applies to every person and every system that talks to a customer on our behalf. Not a
final price, not an indicative price, not a range, not "roughly around". No number.

The reason is not caution for its own sake. Zari rates move with silver and copper commodity
rates. A number given casually on Monday is wrong by Thursday, and a buyer who was told a
figure will hold us to it. Every price we have ever lost money on was a price somebody gave
without checking.

What to do instead when a buyer asks for a rate: collect what is needed for a real quotation —
product, grade, count, shade, quantity, delivery city — confirm we will send a written quotation,
and pass it to Priya.

## Minimum order quantities

| Product | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Zari thread (all grades) | 5 kg per shade |
| Embroidery laces | 90 metres per design (10 rolls) |
| Borders | 90 metres per design (10 rolls) |
| Sequins and tikki trims | 2 kg per shade |
| Cutdana and beads | 2 kg per shade |
| Custom job work | 25 kg |

Minimums are **per shade or per design**, not per total order. A buyer taking three shades of
zari thread needs 5 kg of each, so 15 kg total. This is the most common misunderstanding and
should be explained clearly, not assumed.

## How pricing is structured

Pricing is **per unit against a written quotation** — per kilogram for thread, sequins and
cutdana, per metre for laces and borders. It is never a fixed published price list.

Quantity tiers:

- 5 to 24 kg — standard rate
- 25 to 99 kg — wholesale rate
- 100 kg and above — negotiated, owner approval

All quoted rates are **exclusive of GST and freight**.

GST: 5% on zari thread, 12% on trims, laces, borders, sequins and cutdana.

## Quotation validity

Seven days from the date of issue. After seven days the rate must be re-confirmed because the
metal rate will have moved.

## Discounts

Maximum 3%. Owner approval required in every case.

Discounts are never offered proactively. If a buyer does not ask, we do not raise it. If a buyer
does ask, the request goes to Rakesh — it is not a decision anyone else makes, and it is
certainly not a decision a system makes automatically.

## Below-minimum enquiries

A buyer asking for less than the minimum is not rejected. Explain the minimum, offer the free
shade card, and keep the lead on file. Boutiques often start below minimum and grow.
