# Customers, Buyer Segments and Geography

## Who buys from us today

Five segments, roughly in order of revenue contribution.

**1. Saree wholesalers** — Surat, Mumbai, Varanasi, Kolkata.
Our largest segment. They buy imitation zari and borders in volume, repeat through a season,
and are the most rate-sensitive. They will switch supplier over shade inconsistency faster than
over price.

**2. Garment and lehenga manufacturers** — Surat, Delhi, Jaipur.
Higher value per order, real zari and heavy borders. Their binding constraint is dispatch date —
a late delivery breaks their production schedule, so reliability beats rate for them.

**3. Embroidery job workers** — Surat, Bareilly, Lucknow.
Small, frequent orders. Thread, sequins and cutdana together. They buy from stock and need
same-week availability more than they need range.

**4. Boutiques and designers** — Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad.
Lowest volume, highest margin. They want shade range, bridal-grade real zari, and often want
quantities near our minimum. Worth serving because they order year-round and refer others.

**5. Trim distributors** — Delhi, Ahmedabad, Chennai.
Buy across the full range in bulk and resell locally. The segment most likely to ask for credit
terms, and the segment where credit approval matters most.

## Who we want but do not yet serve

**Exporters.** We would like to supply exporters and we get enquiries from them, but we do not
currently export and we have not set up for it. Any export enquiry goes to Rakesh directly.

## Who we do not sell to

- Retail customers and individuals wanting a few pieces
- Students and hobbyists
- Anyone wanting less than our minimum order quantity
- Buyers outside India

We turn these down politely. A hobbyist today is not a customer, but rudeness travels in this
market and Surat is small.

## Geography

We supply **India only**.

Priority states for new business, in order:
Gujarat, Maharashtra, Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu.

Gujarat and Maharashtra are where we are strongest and where delivery is fastest.
Delhi NCR and Rajasthan are where we have the most room to grow.

## What our ideal customer looks like

A B2B buyer in one of our five segments, located in a priority state, ordering at or above our
minimum quantities, who reorders through a season rather than buying once. They value shade
consistency and reliable dispatch enough to pay a mid-market rate for it.

The clearest tell is a buyer who asks about consistency across batches before they ask about
price. That buyer stays for years.
