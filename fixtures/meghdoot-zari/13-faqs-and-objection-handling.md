# FAQs and Objection Handling

## Frequently asked

**"Are you a manufacturer or a trader?"**
Both, depending on the product. We manufacture zari thread in our own Katargam unit. Laces,
borders, sequins and cutdana we source from partner units and quality-check ourselves.

**"What is your minimum order?"**
5 kg per shade for zari thread, 90 metres per design for laces and borders, 2 kg per shade for
sequins and cutdana, 25 kg for custom job work. Minimums are per shade or design, not per order.

**"Can I get the catalogue?"**
Yes — share the catalogue PDF once the buyer has given their business name and city.

**"Do you give samples?"**
Shade card free, customer pays courier. Cut samples up to 100 g free per shade, maximum
3 shades.

**"What is the rate?"**
Do not answer with a number. Collect grade, count, shade, quantity and delivery city, and tell
them Priya will send a written quotation.

**"How long is delivery?"**
Gujarat 2–3 working days, metros 3–5, rest of India 4–7 — counted from payment confirmation.

**"What payment do you take?"**
NEFT, RTGS, UPI. Cheque only for approved credit accounts. No cash on delivery.

**"Do you export?"**
No. We supply within India only. Escalate the enquiry to Rakesh.

**"Will the shade match my previous lot?"**
Up to 5% variation between batches is normal. For an exact match across a season, order the
full requirement in one lot — say this before the order, not after.

## Objections

**"Your price is too high."**
Do not defend with a number and do not offer a discount. Explain what the price is buying:
real versus imitation zari, plating thickness, batch-to-batch shade consistency. Offer a sample so
they can compare against what they are using now. Then pass it to Priya. If they push on rate,
that is a Rakesh decision, and the maximum is 3%.

**"How do I know your quality is good?"**
Offer the free shade card — it answers the question better than any claim. Mention 16 years in
Surat and 42 people. Offer a customer reference if they want one. Do not promise anything
beyond what the replacement policy actually covers.

**"I need it in 2 days."**
Only within Gujarat is 2–3 working days possible. Anywhere else, say honestly what the window
is. Do not promise faster to win the order. If it is genuinely urgent, escalate to Priya to check
whether anything can be done — but do not commit on her behalf.

**"Can you do just 1 kg?"**
Below our minimum. Explain the 5 kg per shade minimum politely, offer the shade card, and keep
them on file. Small buyers grow, and rudeness travels in this market.

**"Give me 60 days credit."**
Escalate to Nilesh. Do not say yes, do not say it is likely, do not describe what the terms
might be.

**"Your competitor is giving it cheaper."**
Do not name them, do not comment on them, do not match a rate. Restate what we compete on —
consistency and dispatch reliability — and pass it to Priya.
