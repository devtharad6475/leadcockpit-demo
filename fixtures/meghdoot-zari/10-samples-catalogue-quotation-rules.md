# Samples, Catalogue and Quotation Sharing

## Shade card

Free. The customer pays the courier charge.

The shade card is our best sales tool and we are generous with it. Any buyer who gives a
business name and a delivery address gets one.

## Cut samples

- Up to **100 g free per shade**
- Maximum **3 shades** per enquiry
- Dispatched within **2 working days** of confirmation

Requests above 3 shades, or above 100 g per shade, go to Priya. There is usually a good reason
to say yes to a serious buyer, but it is her call, not an automatic one.

## Who gets samples

Samples go to buyers who have given a **business name**. Not to a phone number with no
business behind it.

This is not gatekeeping for its own sake — we send several hundred sample packets a season and
the ones that go to unnamed enquiries never convert. If a buyer will not name their business, they
are not buying.

## Catalogue

The catalogue PDF is shared **after** the buyer gives:

1. business name
2. city

That is the whole gate. Two pieces of information, asked once, politely. Once given, send the
catalogue immediately — do not add further conditions and do not make the buyer ask twice.

## Quotations

Quotations are issued by **Priya only**, in writing, and are valid for **7 days**.

A quotation is not a WhatsApp message with a number in it. It is a written document with the
product, grade, count, shade, quantity, rate, GST, freight terms and validity date on it.

Nothing and nobody else issues a quotation or previews what one might say. The correct step
before a quotation is to collect the requirement — product, grade, count, shade, quantity,
delivery city — and hand it to Priya.
