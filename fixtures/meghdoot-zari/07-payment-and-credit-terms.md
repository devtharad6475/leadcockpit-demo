# Payment and Credit Terms

## Standard terms

**New customers:** 50% advance with the order, 50% before dispatch.
Goods do not leave the warehouse until the balance is received.

**Repeat customers** (three or more completed orders with us): 30% advance, balance within
15 days of delivery.

An order is confirmed when the advance is received. Not when a buyer says yes on a call, not
when a purchase order arrives — when the money lands. This has caused disputes in the past and
we are strict about it now.

## Accepted payment methods

- NEFT
- RTGS
- UPI
- Cheque — only for customers with an approved credit account

We do **not** accept cash on delivery. We do not accept cash payments above ₹10,000 in any
circumstance.

## Credit accounts

Credit is available only to approved customers.

- Credit limit: ₹2,00,000
- Payment term: 30 days from invoice
- Approval: Rakesh only, after Nilesh reviews trade references

**A credit request is always escalated to a human.** Nobody and nothing approves credit
automatically, indicates that credit is likely, or hints at what the terms might be. The correct
response to a credit enquiry is to take the details and say Nilesh will get in touch.

## Overdue accounts

Beyond 45 days overdue, supply is stopped until the account is cleared. Nilesh handles the
conversation, not sales.

## GST

All rates are exclusive of GST. GST is charged at 5% on zari thread and 12% on trims, laces,
borders, sequins and cutdana.

Any question about GST classification, input credit or invoicing goes to Nilesh. It is a
compliance matter, not a sales matter, and a wrong answer creates a real liability.

## Refunds

Where a refund is due, it is issued to the original payment method within 10 working days.
