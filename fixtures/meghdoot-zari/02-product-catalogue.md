# Product Catalogue

We sell six categories. Everything below is B2B, sold in trade quantities.

---

## 1. Zari thread (manufactured in-house)

The core of the business. Three grades:

**Real zari** — silver wire, gold-plated or silver-finished, wound on a silk or cotton core.
Used for high-value bridal and heritage work. Highest cost, highest lustre, does not tarnish
quickly when stored correctly.

**Imitation zari** — copper-based wire with a metallic finish. The volume seller. Suits everyday
saree, lehenga and dress-material work where cost matters but appearance still has to hold up.

**Metallic / plastic zari** — polyester metallised film. Lightest and cheapest, best for
fast-fashion and bulk embroidery runs.

Varieties within the grades: MX metallic, Tikki, Kasab, Sitara.

Counts available: 100/2, 150/2, 200/2.

Sold by: kilogram.

## 2. Embroidery laces

Machine-made laces for garment edging and panel work. Widths from 12 mm to 90 mm.
Available in matching shades to our zari range so a buyer can order thread and lace together
without shade mismatch.

Sold by: metre. Standard roll = 9 metres.

## 3. Borders (saree and lehenga)

Heavier woven borders, 50 mm to 150 mm wide, in zari, resham and mixed compositions.
Our largest design library — over 200 running designs.

Sold by: metre. Standard roll = 9 metres.

## 4. Sequins and tikki trims

Loose sequins, tikki and stone-work trims in assorted sizes and finishes.
Supplied loose by weight, not on string.

Sold by: kilogram.

## 5. Cutdana and beads

Glass and plastic cutdana, bugle beads and assorted embroidery beads.

Sold by: kilogram.

## 6. Custom job work

Dyeing and plating to customer specification. Customer supplies the requirement — shade
reference, count, finish — and we produce to it.

Sold by: kilogram, minimum 25 kg.

---

## Customisation

We can customise:

- shade — matched to a physical swatch or a Pantone reference
- count — within our 100/2 to 200/2 range
- roll length — non-standard roll lengths on request for laces and borders
- packaging and branding — plain or customer-branded packing on larger orders

We cannot customise:

- alloy composition of the zari wire — this is fixed by our plating line

Custom shade matching is available on orders of 25 kg and above.

## Stock and shade range

We hold 60+ standard shades in stock across the zari range. Standard shades ship from stock.
Custom shades are produced to order and add to the delivery timeline — the sales team confirms
the revised timeline at the time of quotation.
