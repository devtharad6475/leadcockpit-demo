# Legal, Privacy and Terms

## Terms of sale

Our terms of sale are printed on every invoice. The material points:

- Goods remain our property until payment is received in full
- Timelines are estimates from payment confirmation, not guarantees against transit delay
- Shade variation up to 5% between batches is normal and not a defect
- Claims for damage, wrong or defective goods must be made within 7 days of delivery
- Returns are not accepted on correct goods delivered as ordered

## Jurisdiction

All disputes are subject to **Surat jurisdiction**.

## Customer data

We collect a customer's business name, contact person, phone number, email, delivery address
and GSTIN. We use it for one purpose: processing and delivering their orders, and following up
on their enquiries.

We do not sell customer data. We do not share it with third parties except the transport company
or courier handling that customer's own consignment.

## Consent for marketing

We do not send marketing or promotional messages without explicit consent. Consent is recorded
against the customer's record with the date it was given.

An enquiry is not consent to marketing. A buyer asking about rates has agreed to a conversation
about their enquiry, not to a broadcast list.

## Opt-out

Honoured immediately and permanently. Once a customer opts out, no further marketing message
goes to them, ever, from any campaign. Order-related messages about an active order continue.

## GST and compliance

GST classification, input credit and invoicing questions go to Nilesh. Nobody else answers
them — a wrong answer on a tax question creates a real liability for the buyer and for us.
