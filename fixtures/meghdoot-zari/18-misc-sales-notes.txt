things buyers keep asking that we never wrote down properly:

- whether we can supply on Amazon / Flipkart - NO, we are not doing marketplace
- whether we do embroidery job work on garments - NO, we only do dyeing and plating
  job work on thread, not embroidery on finished garments. people confuse this.
- whether the shade card is same as catalogue - NO. shade card is physical shades.
  catalogue is the PDF with designs and product list.
- whether we hold stock or make to order - both. 60+ standard shades from stock,
  custom shades made to order.

Surat market notes:

Ring Road is the wholesale hub, most of our walk-in trade comes from there.
Katargam is where the production units are.
Most brokers in the market work on commission from the buyer side, not seller side.
Broker enquiries should be treated like any other - ask for the end buyer's business
name and city.

transport companies we use:

- for Gujarat and Maharashtra - local transport, freight to pay
- for north - Delhi/Jaipur route transport
- small parcels under 20 kg - courier, we pay and add to invoice unless order is
  above 50000 in which case freight is free
