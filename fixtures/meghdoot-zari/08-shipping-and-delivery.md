# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery timelines

| Destination | Working days |
|---|---|
| Within Gujarat | 2–3 |
| Metro cities — Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai | 3–5 |
| Rest of India | 4–7 |

These timelines run from **payment confirmation**, not from the date of enquiry and not from the
date the order was discussed. This distinction matters and should be stated when a timeline is
given, because buyers routinely count from the wrong day and then believe we were late.

**Nobody promises a date faster than the published window.** If a buyer needs it faster than
this, the honest answer is that we cannot, and the enquiry goes to Priya to see whether anything
is possible. We would rather lose the order than miss the date.

Custom shades are produced to order and fall outside these windows. The revised timeline is
confirmed in the quotation.

## How we ship

- Orders above 20 kg — by transport company, freight to pay
- Orders up to 20 kg — by courier, prepaid

Freight is free on orders above ₹50,000 within India.

## Dispatch schedule

Dispatch cut-off is 4:00 PM IST. Orders cleared after the cut-off dispatch the next working day.

No dispatch on Sundays or public holidays.

## Tracking

The transport docket number or courier tracking number is shared on the day of dispatch.
If a buyer asks where their goods are and we have already dispatched, share the docket number.
If we have not dispatched, say so plainly with the expected dispatch date — do not guess.

## Damage in transit

Goods are inspected and packed before dispatch. Transit damage must be reported within
7 days of delivery with photographs, and is handled under the replacement policy.

## Coverage

We supply within **India only**. We do not currently export.
