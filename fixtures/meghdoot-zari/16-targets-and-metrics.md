# Targets and Success Metrics

These are internal targets. They are not shared with customers.

## Lead and conversion targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Qualified leads per month | 40 |
| Enquiry → sample conversion | 25% |
| Sample → order conversion | 20% |
| Average order value | ₹85,000 |
| First-response time, working hours | under 15 minutes |

## What we actually watch

**Response time** is the metric that moves everything else. In this trade a buyer messages four
suppliers at once and buys from whoever answers first with something useful. Fifteen minutes is
not an aspiration, it is the difference between winning and not being in the conversation.

**Sample-to-order conversion** tells us whether we are sending samples to the right people. If it
drops below 20%, we are sending samples too freely and should tighten qualification, not send
more samples.

**Repeat rate within a season** tells us whether shade consistency is holding. A wholesaler who
buys once and does not come back in the same season almost always had a shade problem.

## Seasonality in the numbers

Do not read a lean-season month against a peak-season target. August to November should carry
roughly double the lead volume of May to July. A flat 40 leads every month is the annual
average, not a monthly expectation.
