# Internal Operations and Escalation

## Who does what

| Person | Role | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| Rakesh Meghani | Owner | Pricing beyond policy, all discounts, credit approval, export enquiries, anything unusual |
| Priya Shah | Sales head | All quotations, sample requests beyond standard, urgent delivery requests, negotiation |
| Nilesh Patel | Accounts | Payments, credit account setup, overdue accounts, GST and invoicing, refunds |

## Escalation path

Agent or sales staff → **Priya within 2 hours** → **Rakesh same day**.

Nothing waits overnight without someone knowing about it.

## Service standards

- First response to a new enquiry: under 15 minutes during working hours
- Complaint acknowledgement: within 2 hours during working hours
- Complaint resolution: within 3 working days
- Quotation issued: within 1 working day of the requirement being complete
- Sample dispatch: within 2 working days of confirmation

## Order confirmation

An order is confirmed when the advance payment is received. Not on a verbal yes, not on a
purchase order. Production is not scheduled before the advance lands.

## Working hours

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 IST. Closed Sundays and public holidays.
Dispatch cut-off 4:00 PM IST.
