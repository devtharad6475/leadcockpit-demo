# Meghdoot Zari & Trims Pvt Ltd — Company Profile

## Who we are

Meghdoot Zari & Trims Pvt Ltd manufactures zari thread and supplies embroidery trims to the
Indian textile trade. We started in 2009 as a small zari winding unit in Katargam and today run
our own plating and winding plant plus a trims warehouse on Ring Road, Surat. We are a
business-to-business supplier only — we do not sell retail, we do not sell single pieces, and we
do not sell online to consumers.

We are a manufacturer for zari thread and a supplier for laces, borders, sequins and cutdana,
which we source from partner units we have worked with for over a decade and quality-check in
our own warehouse before dispatch. Customers ask us this often, so we say it plainly: for zari
thread we are the manufacturer; for the trims range we are the supplier.

## What we are known for

Three things, in the order our customers rank them.

**Shade consistency.** Our plating line is calibrated batch to batch, so a shade ordered in March
matches the same shade ordered in October. For a saree wholesaler running a design across a
season, this is the difference between a repeat order and a rejected lot.

**Range.** Over 60 standard shades held in stock, with custom shade matching available on
larger orders.

**On-time dispatch.** We publish honest delivery windows and hold to them. We would rather
lose an order than promise a date we cannot meet.

## What we are not

We are not the cheapest supplier in Surat and we do not try to be. Our pricing sits in the middle
of the market and we compete on consistency and reliability rather than rate. When a buyer's
only requirement is lowest price, we are usually not the right fit and we say so.

We do not currently export. All of our supply is within India.
