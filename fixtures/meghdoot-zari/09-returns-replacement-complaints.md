# Returns, Replacement and Complaints

## Returns

**We do not accept returns on correct goods delivered as ordered.**

Zari and trims are cut, wound and shade-matched to order. Once a lot has left our warehouse we
cannot resell it as fresh stock. This is standard in the trade and buyers understand it, but it has
to be said clearly at the time of order rather than argued about afterwards.

## Replacement

We replace goods that are **damaged, wrong, or defective**.

Conditions:

- Reported within **7 days of delivery**
- Photographs of the goods and the packing provided
- Goods in original packing, not cut or used

Replacement is dispatched once we have verified the claim. Where replacement is not possible,
a refund is issued to the original payment method within 10 working days.

## Shade variation

Shade variation of up to **5%** between batches is normal in zari and is **not** treated as a
defect. This is a property of the plating process, not a quality failure.

We tell buyers this before the order, not after the complaint. A buyer running a design across
a season should order the full season's requirement in one lot if exact match matters to them —
we will say this proactively rather than let them discover it.

## Custom and job-work orders

No return and no replacement on custom or job-work orders, except for a genuine manufacturing
defect. The goods are made to the customer's own specification and cannot be resold.

## Cancellation

- Before dispatch: allowed
- After dispatch: not allowed
- Custom orders once production has started: not allowed

Where an advance has been taken on a cancelled order, the refund position depends on how far
production has gone. Nilesh decides this case by case.

## How complaints are handled

**Every complaint goes to a human.** Acknowledge it, record what happened, and escalate. Nobody
adjudicates a complaint on the spot, offers compensation, admits fault, or tells a buyer their
claim is invalid.

Service standard:

- Acknowledge within **2 hours** during working hours
- Resolve within **3 working days**

An angry customer who is acknowledged quickly usually stays a customer. One who is argued
with does not.
